## THE TRUST PROBLEM (Why This Matters)

### What Can Go Wrong
```
1. Paid shills (someone paid to promote coin)
2. Pump+dump schemes (artificially inflate then dump)
3. Bots (fake accounts amplifying fake news)
4. Emotional traders (yelling but no skill)
5. Market manipulation (coordinated FUD or FOMO)
6. Outdated info (yesterday's news for today's trade)
7. Conflicts of interest (they own the coin they promote)
```

### How We Validate
```
1. Cross-check: Is this news on Bloomberg/CoinDesk too?
2. Profile check: How long has this person been active?
3. Consistency check: Do their past predictions come true?
4. Verification check: Is account verified + real?
5. Bias check: Do they promote their own coins?
6. Timing check: Is this real-time or old info?
7. Track record check: Can we measure their accuracy?
```

### 3. THE SWEET SPOT: PRICE ACTION LEADS, NEWS EXPLAINS

**Key academic insight**: Big players (banks, hedge funds) position BEFORE news drops, creating price action patterns that predict the move.

**How it works**:
```
Day 1: Big players see upcoming news → start positioning
       Price action shows unusual patterns (wedges, breakouts)
       
Day 2-3: Price moves significantly based on their positions
         
Day 4: News drops → market "reacts" to news
       
Reality: Market already priced it in (price action led)
```

**Evidence**: "Price action patterns sometimes form BEFORE big moves because big players were getting ready for the upcoming move based on their interpretation of how the news might affect the market" (PriceAction.com)

## EFFICIENT MARKET HYPOTHESIS (EMH): THE CONTEXT

### What EMH says:
"Prices incorporate all available information → only news moves prices → random walk"

### Why it's WRONG (modern view):
```
1. Momentum exists (market anomaly EMH can't explain)
2. Short-term serial correlations are NOT zero (EMH predicts they should be)
3. Markets undershoot/overshoot news (don't instantly price in)
4. Big players create patterns BEFORE news (price action leads)
5. Emotional factors cause persistent mispricing
```

**Academic consensus**: "EMH does NOT work. Empirical research shows momentum effects and patterns that shouldn't exist if EMH were true" (CFAInstitute 2003, McCracken 2025)

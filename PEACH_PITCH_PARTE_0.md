## POR QUÉ FUNCIONA (½ página)

**The Revenue Alignment Model:**

Traditional SaaS:
- User pays $50/month
- Company profits whether user succeeds or fails
- Result: Low engagement, high churn (95%)

PEACH Model:
- Trader pays $3-5K/month + 20% of profits
- PEACH profits ONLY if trader profits
- Result: Perfect alignment

Example math:
```
Marcus makes $30K/month → Pays us $5K + $6K = $11K/month
Marcus nets $19K/month → Feels amazing
We get $11K → We're incentivized to keep him profitable
```

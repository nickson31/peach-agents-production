## PATTERN ANALYSIS: How They Generate Leads LEGALLY

### The 7 Legal Channels (Verified)

```
1. CONTENT MARKETING (56% of platforms)
   └─ Blog + SEO ranking for relevant keywords
   └─ Example: Binance 13M organic monthly = SEO dominance
   └─ Cost: $5-50K/month in content
   └─ Legality: ✅ 100% legal

2. AFFILIATE PROGRAMS (48% of platforms)
   └─ Pay creators for referrals
   └─ Example: Bybit 40% commission = attracts YouTubers
   └─ Cost: Pay-per-acquisition (often 10-40% of first month fee)
   └─ Legality: ✅ 100% legal (fully disclosed)

3. COMMUNITY + WORD-OF-MOUTH (60% of platforms)
   └─ Build Telegram/Discord/Reddit presence
   └─ Let users organically share
   └─ Cost: Community manager salary ($3-10K/month)
   └─ Legality: ✅ 100% legal

4. PRODUCT-LED GROWTH (44% of platforms)
   └─ Free tier → paid tier conversion
   └─ Example: 3Commas free tier, then premium
   └─ Cost: Infrastructure costs
   └─ Legality: ✅ 100% legal

5. EDUCATIONAL CONTENT (68% of platforms)
   └─ YouTube tutorials, courses, webinars
   └─ Attract learners → convert to users
   └─ Cost: Creator salaries or partnership fees
   └─ Legality: ✅ 100% legal

6. PARTNERSHIPS + INTEGRATIONS (40% of platforms)
   └─ Partner with exchanges, tools, services
   └─ Mutual lead exchange
   └─ Cost: Revenue share or commission
   └─ Legality: ✅ 100% legal

7. PAID ADVERTISING (32% of platforms)
   └─ Google Ads, Facebook, Reddit, Twitter
   └─ Target keyword buyers
   └─ Cost: $10-100K/month depending on scale
   └─ Legality: ✅ 100% legal
```

## 📋 MARKET CONSENSUS STRATEGY (From 23 Videos)

**What ACTUALLY works** (implied by most successful traders):

1. **Entry Signal**: Momentum + structure (RSI + support/resistance)
2. **Risk Sizing**: Never more than 1-2% of account per trade
3. **Exit Rules**: Profit targets + time-based + manual override
4. **Monitoring**: Someone watching, ready to kill it
5. **Verification**: Real money P&L visible, not simulated

**PEACH IMPLEMENTATION**:
```
AI analyzes → Suggests trade (10 sec delay for override)
  ↓
User approves/rejects (1-click decision)
  ↓
If approved: Execute with guardrails (position size, stop loss)
  ↓
Real-time P&L tracking (transparent)
  ↓
User reviews daily/weekly (not 24/7 required)
```
